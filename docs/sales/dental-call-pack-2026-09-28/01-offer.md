# Offer and positioning: the booking receptionist (dental)

## One-line position
"Our receptionist answers your practice's calls in the cover you choose (after hours, overflow when the desk is busy, or alongside your team during business hours). At go-live it books into a connected Google Calendar or Cal.com calendar (a booking request otherwise) and texts the patient a confirmation. Each business's booking, routing and texts are set up and tested before they go live."

Say what it does, then say where it stops. We sell **calls answered in the cover the practice configures, turned into booked appointments or clean hand-offs**. We don't sell it as missed-call cover, and we never promise "more patients" or revenue.

**Call to action, everywhere:** "Book a 15-minute demo."

## How a practice starts
There is no pilot or trial offer. The approved promise is:
> "Each business's booking, routing and texts are set up and tested before they go live."

What that means on a call:
- We run the go-live tests with the practice before go-live: a test booking (or, in Booking request mode, a booking request received), an urgent-wording call, test calls through the configured cover and call forwarding, and a text when SMS is configured.
- **Acceptance** is those tests passing on the practice's own configuration plus the practice's written confirmation. ## Two booking modes (say them identically everywhere in this pack)
Direct booking depends on the practice's system: we confirm compatibility in setup, or agree a booking-request / lead workflow. Never oversell this.

| | **Booked** | **Booking request / lead capture** |
|---|---|---|
| When it applies | Practice runs (or will run) a **connected Google Calendar or Cal.com calendar** | Practice runs closed practice software — e.g. **Dentally, Core Practice, D4W** — that **we don't book into directly today** (see the Dentally note below; we've built no adapter for any of these) |
| What the caller hears | The receptionist checks availability, books the slot, and reads back a **reference number** on the call | The receptionist takes name, number, reason and preferred time; says the team will confirm it, **no reference number, no promise of a time** |
| What the practice does | Nothing — it's already in the calendar | The practice **commits** to confirming each request from the dashboard, e.g. by the **next business morning** (its commitment, not something the receptionist does) |
| Dentally note | Dentally **does publish a public developer API** (patients, appointments, availability — `https://developer.dentally.co/`, checked 27 Sep 2026) that could support direct booking later. **Marked possible future direct booking. Not offered today.** Don't say it's coming; say it's not built. | — |

Ask which system they use **before** describing either mode (`10-qualification.md` §"Which system

## The calendar reality (qualify this early)
It books into **Google Calendar or Cal.com**. Most dental practices book in practice software. Two honest options:
1. They already run a Google or Cal.com calendar for some appointment types (new-patient consults, cosmetic consults): at go-live it books into that connected calendar — **Booked** mode.
2. They give the receptionist a **dedicated booking calendar** with the slots they're happy to release; the team copies each booking into their practice software (the email alert tells them to) — this is still **Booked** mode, on the dedicated calendar. If they won't accept either a connected calendar or a dedicated one, they're **Booking request** mode at best, or not a fit yet. Say so.

## What we must never say
- "You'll never miss a call" / "capture every enquiry" / any revenue, patient-number or ROI promise (ACCC misleading conduct). Never position it as missed-call cover.
- Any pilot, free trial, "risk-free" start or "nothing owing" offer. None is approved.
- A setup price, or any setup invoicing. Setup is quoted separately once approved.
- "It's live and booking for other practices." We have no receptionist client yet.
- "It plugs into Dentally / Core Practice / Cliniko."
- "It'll put callers through to you." Transfer isn't offered.
- "It can tell whether it's an emergency." It routes urgent wording to 000; it never assesses.
- That call data stays in Australia. Retell, Twilio and the language model provider process calls overseas, including in the US (APP 8).
- Anything about patient testimonials or outcomes (National Law s 133, Ahpra).
