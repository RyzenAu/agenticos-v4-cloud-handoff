AI RECEPTIONIST

The calls your front desk can't get to, answered.

An AI receptionist for dental practices and other appointment-based businesses. It answers after-hours and overflow calls, handles the common questions, takes messages and booking requests, and hands the caller to your staff. Your practice gets a summary of every call.

After hours and overflow. Calls you can't pick up are answered instead of going to voicemail.
Common questions. Hours, parking, health funds, first visits: only from facts you approve.
Messages and booking requests. Name, number, reason and preferred times, for your team to confirm.
Hand-over to staff. Anyone who asks for a person is transferred or flagged for a call back.
A summary of every call. Who rang, why, and what needs doing, sent to the practice.

Where we are: a working system, tested with synthetic calls. We haven't yet run it on a real practice's phones, and we're looking for our first pilot practices.

HOW IT WORKS

The full five-stage process →
STEP 1
We build your concept site

From what's already public about your business — your name, services and location — we design and build an unofficial concept site. No conversation required, no cost, no obligation.

STEP 2
You review it

We send you the link. Walk through it on your own phone, on your own time. If it's not for you, that's the end of it — nothing was ever asked of you.

STEP 3
We build the real thing

If you like what you see, we have a conversation about your business specifically, agree the scope and timeline in writing, and build your actual site — live in weeks.

A guide for the visitor who lands on your site at 9:40pm.

The website assistant is a chat window on your site. It helps visitors get an answer, guides them to book, enquire or request an appraisal, and escalates to a human whenever it can’t confirm something itself.
