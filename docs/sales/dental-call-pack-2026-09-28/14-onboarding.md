**Precondition:** booking and SMS go-live has been approved by Usman and passed the 5 retest calls on our demo line. Until then, a signed client waits at step 0; monthly billing starts only at Acceptance (step 8).

| Step | What happens | Who | Typical time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Proposal accepted; agreement signed **after qualified legal review of the template** | Client + Usman | — |
| 1 | 45-minute kickoff: hours (incl. public holidays), services and lengths, what may be booked by phone, urgent-call wording, who gets alerts | Client + M&U | Day 1 |
| 2 | Client completes the setup checklist (no passwords, no patient details in it) | Client | Days 1–3 |
| 3 | Client grants calendar access (Google Calendar or Cal.com) from their own account | Client | Days 1–3 |
| 4 | We configure the receptionist: prompt from the generator, knowledge summary (hours, published fees, policies), disclosures, per-period billing rounding, SMS consent wording | M&U | Days 2–5 |
| 5 | Client privacy policy updated: AI receptionist, recording, overseas providers (APP 1, 5, 8). We supply the wording; they publish it | Client | before go-live |
| 6 | Number set up; client switches call forwarding on in the agreed cover (after hours / overflow / all calls alongside the team where the package includes it). Until Acceptance the line only takes messages. Rollback: switch it off | Client | Day 5 |
| 7 | Go-live tests, 5 scripted test calls together: a booking that appears in their calendar (or, in Booking request mode, a booking request received), an urgent-wording call, calls through the configured cover and forwarding, and a text when SMS is configured | M&U + client | Days 5–7 |
| 8 | **Acceptance:** the tests pass on the client's configuration and the client confirms in writing. Monthly billing and the minimum term start. **Timeline promise:** "About a week from kickoff to your first test booking, once we have calendar access and your answers." Never promise a date before step 0's precondition is met.

## What we never ask for
Passwords (calendar access is granted from their account), patient lists, clinical notes, or API keys by email.
