WHAT A GOOD SITE NEEDS

What a dental practice site has to get right.
Booking that works from a phone

An appointment request that takes under a minute to fill in, with the practice's hours and accepted health funds visible before anyone has to ask.

Hours, location and emergency info up front

Opening hours, address, parking and what to do about a dental emergency belong on the home page, not three clicks deep.

Compliance-safe copy

Ahpra's advertising guidelines restrict outcome claims and testimonials about clinical results. We write persuasive copy that stays inside those rules.

COMPLIANCE-SAFE COPY

Ahpra advertising guidelines

The National Law prohibits misleading claims, guarantees of results, and testimonials that imply a clinical outcome. We describe services, qualifications and facilities factually, keep before/after and outcome language out of marketing copy, and avoid fear-based claims — so the site reads as trustworthy and stays inside Ahpra's rules.
