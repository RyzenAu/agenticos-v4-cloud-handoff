# Qualification: is this practice a fit?

**Ask "which system do you use" first, before anything else on this list** — it decides which
booking mode (`01-offer.md` §"Two booking modes") you can honestly offer, and every later question
is asked in that light. Question 2 can be asked on the cold call. Log each answer in the CRM
note. **A "no" on any must-have means not a fit yet**: say so kindly and log **Closed – not a
fit** with the reason.

## Question 0: which system, and which booking mode follows

| They say | Booking mode you can offer |
|---|---|
| Google Calendar or Cal.com already, for any appointment type | **Booked** |
| Dentally, Core Practice, D4W or similar practice software, **and** willing to run a dedicated Google/Cal.com calendar alongside it for released slots | **Booked**, on the dedicated calendar (§"The calendar reality") |
| Dentally, Core Practice, D4W or similar, **not** willing to run a separate calendar | **Booking request / lead capture** only — say so before the demo, not after they've signed |
| Something else / unsure | Find out before quoting either mode |

Dentally publishes a public developer API (`https://developer.dentally.co/`, checked 27 Sep
2026) that could support direct booking later — say "not built yet" if asked, never "coming soon".

## Must-haves

| # | Question | Fit | Not a fit yet |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Which calls would you want answered and booked: after hours, overflow when the desk is busy, or all calls alongside your team?" | At least one cover mode they want (after hours, overflow, or all calls; every package covers all three) | They want none of those covered |
| 2 | "Where do bookings live?" | Google Calendar or Cal.com already, **or** willing to release some appointment types into a dedicated booking calendar and copy bookings into their practice software | Practice software only, and unwilling to use a separate calendar — **still a fit for Booking request / lead capture**, just say which mode they're getting |
| 3 | "Which appointments may someone book without your team?" | At least one type (e.g. new-patient check-up, clean, cosmetic consult) with a fixed length | "Nothing can be booked without a clinician deciding" |
| 4 | "Are you comfortable with callers hearing an automated assistant that says so?" | Yes | No |
| 5 | "Can your privacy policy name overseas service providers?" | Yes (we supply wording; APP 1, 5, 8) | No |
| 6 | "Who decides, and who'd be our contact day to day?" | Named decision-maker and a named day-to-day contact | Nobody can decide |
| 7 | "Can you change call forwarding with your phone provider?" | Yes, or they know who can | No control over their phone line |

## Nice to know (sizes the package)

| Question | Points to |
|---|---|
| Calls a week they'd want the receptionist to take, in the cover they chose (their number) | Compare with each package's included minutes (`11-package-comparison.md`); several locations → Premium |
| How many practitioners take bookings | 1 → Essential; 2–3 → Professional; 4+ or multi-site → Premium |
| Do they want reminder texts? | Yes → Professional or Premium |
| Do they want someone to review calls weekly? | Yes → Premium |
| Current online booking (HotDoc, HealthEngine) | Keep it; we cover phone callers |

## Red flags (pause and check with Usman)
- They want it to give clinical advice, triage, quote treatment prices we haven't put in the knowledge summary, or transfer calls. We don't.
- They want a guaranteed number of bookings or a revenue promise.
- They want it answering **all** calls on day one at a busy multi-chair practice before its booking, routing and texts have been set up and tested on overflow first.
- Anyone asks us to use patient data from their system for marketing.
