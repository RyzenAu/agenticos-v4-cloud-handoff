AI RECEPTIONIST

The calls your front desk can’t get to, answered.

An AI receptionist for dental practices and other appointment-based businesses. It answers after-hours and overflow calls, handles the common questions, takes messages and booking requests, and hands the caller to your staff. Your practice gets a summary of every call.

WHAT IT DOES

Five jobs, done the same way every time.
After hours and overflow

Your number stays the same. Calls you can't pick up, because it's after hours or the desk is busy, forward to the receptionist instead of voicemail. You choose when it answers, and forwarding can be switched off at any time.

Common questions

Hours, location, parking, health funds, how to prepare for a first visit. It answers only from facts your practice has approved. If it doesn't know, it says so and takes a message.

Messages and booking requests

It takes the caller's name, number and reason in their own words, and records a booking request with the times that suit them. Your team confirms the appointment.

Hand-over to staff

A caller who asks for a person gets one: a transfer during opening hours, or a message flagged for a call back. A caller never has to argue with it to reach someone.

A summary of every call

Each call ends with a short written summary for the practice: who rang, why, what was promised and what needs doing. Nothing is left in a voicemail box.

DISCLOSURE FIRST

The first thing every caller hears.

The opening says plainly that it's an AI and that the call may be recorded, before anything else is said. In NSW, recording a private conversation needs the consent of everyone on it; announcing it first and letting the caller choose to continue is how we meet that. Your practice approves the exact wording.

“Hi, you've reached the practice. I'm the practice's AI assistant, and this call may be recorded and transcribed. How can I help?”

DENTAL PRACTICES

For dental practices: it routes, it never advises.
No clinical advice, no diagnosis, no medication advice — ever.
If a caller describes something life-threatening, such as swelling that affects breathing or bleeding that won't stop, it tells them to hang up and call 000.
Dental emergencies go to your team during opening hours, or to your after-hours arrangement.
It asks only what the booking needs, not a clinical history.

WHAT IT WON’T DO

It won't pretend to be a person.
It won't quote a fee your practice hasn't given it.
It won't make outbound calls or send marketing.
It won't confirm an appointment in your diary on its own; your team does.

AT YOUR PRACTICE

What changes at your front desk, and what doesn't.
Your number stays yours

Forwarding is set on your own phone line for the calls you choose: after hours, busy, or no answer. Removing it puts everything back as it was.

Hand-overs go where you say

You nominate who takes a transfer in opening hours and who is contacted about urgent calls after hours. Your team still confirms every appointment.

If the service is unavailable

Forwarding is set up so a call it can't take falls back to your existing voicemail, rather than ringing out.

When it isn't sure

It doesn't guess. After one failed attempt to understand a caller, it hands over or takes a message for your team.

PRIVACY

Your callers' information.

The detail goes in writing with your proposal. Your practice approves the wording callers hear, and you remain responsible for your own privacy policy.

It asks only what the call needs: name, contact number, the reason in the caller's own words, and how urgent it is. No clinical history.
Recording is off unless your practice chooses to turn it on, and callers are told before anything is captured.
Retention periods for transcripts and recordings are agreed with your practice, and records can be deleted on request.
Some of the services that process calls are based overseas. We name them so your privacy policy can list them, as the Australian Privacy Principles require.
Health service providers are covered by the Privacy Act whatever their size, so we set it up on that basis.

GETTING STARTED

Three steps, and your number never changes.
A call about your practice

Which calls you miss now, your hours, what counts as urgent, and who should take a hand-over.

We set it up and test it

We load your approved answers and hand-over rules, then test it with made-up callers. You listen to test calls and sign off the wording.

You switch on forwarding

Your carrier forwards the calls you choose. Turning it off takes a couple of minutes, and your number never changes.

QUESTIONS

Straight answers.
What does the AI receptionist do?

It answers the calls your team can't get to — after hours and when the front desk is busy. It says it's an AI and that the call may be recorded, answers common questions from facts you've approved, takes messages and booking requests, and hands over to your staff. Your practice gets a summary of every call. For dental practices it routes urgent calls and never gives clinical advice.

Has the AI receptionist been used by a real practice yet?

Not yet. It is a working system we have tested with synthetic calls, and we are looking for our first pilot practices. We would rather tell you that plainly than imply results we don't have. A demo call is available on request.
