CONCEPT STUDY
Lantern Dental

Dental practice concept site designed and developed by M&U Ventures. A fictional business, not a client.

A fictional Rozelle dental clinic with a real online booking system: a six-step flow backed by a live Postgres diary, a database constraint that stops double bookings, and a clinic dashboard to run it from — inside Ahpra's advertising rules.

Home, treatments overview, six treatment pages, meet the team, new patients, fees and payment, FAQs, and contact and location pages
Six-step online booking: new or returning patient, appointment type, practitioner preference, live availability, minimal details, review
Postgres-backed diary with a database constraint that prevents two bookings landing on the same slot
Clinic dashboard (demo access, no password): day calendar, requests queue, availability editing, appointment types, patient list and notification previews

CONCEPT STUDY
Marden & Rowe Solicitors

Law firm concept site designed and developed by M&U Ventures. A fictional business, not a client.

A fictional Leichhardt solicitor and conveyancing firm with a courtroom-led client funnel: a five-step quote request that returns an instant estimate or routes to human review, a diary that won't double-book a consultation, and a demo client portal — inside the Legal Profession Uniform Law's rules.

Home, services, buying, selling, contract review, the conveyancing process, fees and quote request, about and team, FAQs, resources and contact pages
Five-step quote request: matter type, property type, suburb and state, stage and deadline, contact
Instant sample estimate for standard NSW residential matters; automatic routing to human review for anything unusual, with the reason stated
20-minute consultation booking with a diary that refuses an already-taken slot
