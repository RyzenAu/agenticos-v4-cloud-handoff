Inspections, features, the suburb
A three-storey Victorian terrace at the quiet end of Darling Street

Set at the eastern end of Darling Street, a short walk down to the Balmain East wharf, this 1880s terrace has been restored rather than remodelled. The iron-lace balcony, marble fireplaces and the original staircase remain; the kitchen and bathrooms were replaced in 2021 in a way that stays out of the house's way.

The ground floor runs front to back from the formal sitting room through to a kitchen and dining room opening to a paved courtyard. Three bedrooms occupy the first floor, and the attic level has been converted into a study with a dormer window looking towards the city.

Rear lane access provides a single off-street car space, which on this stretch of Darling Street is worth mentioning twice.

Balmain

Balmain sits on a peninsula between Darling Harbour and Iron Cove, ten minutes by ferry from Circular Quay. Its streets run from the Darling Street ridge down to the water on both sides, and most of the housing stock is Victorian: sandstone cottages, workers' terraces and the grander Italianate houses along the eastern end.
