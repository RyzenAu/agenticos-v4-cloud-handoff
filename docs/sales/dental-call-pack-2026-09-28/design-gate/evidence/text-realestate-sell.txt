WHY SELL WITH US

Four things you'll get, in writing.
One agent throughout

The person who appraises your home runs the campaign, hosts every inspection, negotiates and calls the auction.

A written price guide, with the comparables

You see the sales we based the estimate on. If the guide changes during the campaign, you'll know why.

Itemised marketing costs

Every cost is listed in the agency agreement and approved by you. Nothing is added later without your written sign-off.

A report after every open home

Attendance, feedback, contract requests and offers, written down, so you can see how the campaign is going and change course.

PROCESS

From appraisal to settlement.

Six steps, the same for every property. Timeframes depend on the method of sale and are agreed with you at the start.

01
Appraisal

We walk through the property with you, talk about the street and recent sales, and follow up with a written estimate and our recommended method of sale.

02
Agreement and plan

One agency agreement with the estimated selling price, commission and every marketing cost itemised. Nothing is added later without your written agreement.

03
Preparation

Presentation advice, styling if it will make a difference, photography, floor plans and a campaign schedule. We check the light before we set inspection times.

04
Campaign

Open inspections, buyer follow-up within a day, and a written report to you after every open home with attendance, feedback and any offers.

05
Negotiation or auction

Every offer is put to you in writing. On auction day the same agent who appraised your home is on the steps.

06
Exchange to settlement

We stay across the contract, the building and pest reports, and settlement day, and we're still reachable afterwards.

MARKETING

Spent where it changes the result.

Portal presence

Signboard and print
