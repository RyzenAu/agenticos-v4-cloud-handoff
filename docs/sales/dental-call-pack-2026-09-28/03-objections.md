# Objections: honest answers (booking receptionist)

Rule for every answer: **agree with what's true, shrink the ask, offer the demo or leave it.** Never promise a capability that is "at go-live" as if it were live.

| # | They say | You say |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Is this actually live? Who else uses it?" | "Honest answer: you'd be one of our first clients. The receptionist is live on our demo line and takes messages today; booking switches on at go-live once it passes our tests. Each business's booking, routing and texts are set up and tested before they go live, so you'd see your own line book a test appointment before anything goes live." |
| 2 | "An AI answering our patients? No." | "Fair. It only takes the calls you choose: after hours, overflow when everyone's busy, or all calls alongside your team. It tells every caller it's automated. If you'd rather your patients never hear it, it's not for you, and that's fine." |
| 3 | "Does it plug into Dentally / Core Practice / Cliniko?" | "No, and I won't pretend it does. At go-live it books into a connected Google Calendar or Cal.com calendar. The way it works alongside practice software is a dedicated calendar with the slots you're happy to release; your team gets an email for each booking and copies it across. If that's a deal-breaker, better to know now." |
| 4 | "We've got online booking (HotDoc etc.)." | "Good, keep it. This is for people who pick up the phone instead: often older patients and people with a question first. Book a 15-minute demo and see if it fits." |
| 5 | "We've got voicemail." | "Voicemail works when someone calls back quickly. The difference, at go-live and with a connected Google Calendar or Cal.com calendar, is that the caller gets a booked time on the call, plus a text confirming it, instead of waiting. If voicemail is working well for you, genuinely fine." |
| 6 | "What if someone rings with an emergency?" | "It never assesses anyone. Any urgent or life-threatening wording gets 'hang up and call 000' first, then an urgent message to your team. It isn't an emergency service and the agreement says so." |
| 7 | "Can it transfer to us?" | "No. Live transfer to a person isn't offered in any package. It takes a message, or books instead." |
| 8 | "Privacy? Where does the data go?" | "Calls are processed by our voice and phone providers, some overseas, including the US. Your privacy policy needs to say so (APP 8), and we give you the wording. We keep only what's needed for the booking, and we agree retention with you." |
| 9 | "You're two people. Will you be around?" | "We're a two-person Sydney business and I won't dress that up. Support is weekdays 9 to 5; Premium gets a two-hour response. And you can switch forwarding off at any time and your phones work exactly as they do today." |
| 10 | "Too expensive." | "Understood. Essential is A$699 a month + GST, with 400 minutes included. Before you decide, let's work it through on your own numbers: which calls you'd want it to take and what a booked appointment is worth to you. If the maths doesn't work, don't buy it." |
| 11 | "What if it books the wrong thing?" | "It only books the appointment types and slots you release, and only says 'you're booked' when your calendar confirms. Everything else becomes a message. We review how it's going with you after the first two weeks; on Premium we review flagged calls daily for those two weeks." |
| 12 | "How did you get this number? Is this a sales call?" | "Yes, it's a sales call. I'm Usman from M&U Ventures; your number's on your website. If you'd rather I didn't call again, I'll note that now." |
| 13 | "Just email me something." | "Happy to. What's the best email? One short email, and I won't chase unless you ask." |
| 14 | "The principal decides." | "No problem. Would a one-page summary help, or should I call when they're in?" |
| 15 | "Can I ring your demo number now?" | "You can. Today it'll answer, tell you it's automated, and take a message; it won't book yet. If you'd like to hear it book, I'll show you in the demo and send you the number the day booking's switched on." Once the per-prospect demo is live (`05-demo-path.md`), instead: "Actually, ring this number now from your practice phone — you'll hear a demo built from your own website." |
| 16 | "Do I have to change my phone number or move providers?" | "No. Your number stays exactly as it is, with your current carrier. You just dial a short code that says 'if nobody answers, try this number instead' — the same feature your phone already has for voicemail. One more code turns it off." |
| 17 | "Will it actually book, or just take a message for us to sort out?" | "Depends what you run bookings in. If you're on Google Calendar or Cal.com, then once it's set up and tested (at go-live) it books into that connected calendar on the spot and reads back a reference. If you're on Dentally, Core Practice or D4W, we can't write into that system, so it takes the name, number, reason and preferred time, and your desk confirms it: you decide how fast, for example by the next business morning. I'll ask what you run before I promise either." |
| 18 | "We're not dental — do you do this for [a law firm / real estate]?" | "Yes, at the same package prices. It answers, takes down the details and urgency, and at go-live emails your team with a callback list; it books only where you use a connected Google Calendar or Cal.com calendar. Trades we don't do yet. Book a 15-minute demo and I'll show you." (Our demo line still says law firms are "coming soon": demo a law firm on the dental or real-estate flow and say so.) |

**If they ask something you don't know:** "I don't want to guess. I'll check and come back to you by [day]." Write it in the CRM note.
