### 6. What NOT to Copy

* **Do not use generic raw system prompt text:** Showing a massive prompt box with markdown syntax (`## Role`, `## Style`) will overwhelm non-technical small business clients (e.g., clinic managers or plumbers).  
  * *Interpretation:* Replace backend prompt views with polished UI mockups or simplified configuration settings (e.g., "Custom Business FAQs", "Office Hours").
* **Do not present unfinished dashboard states:** The Live Monitoring view at 05:50 displays *"No Ongoing Calls"*, leaving an empty white canvas. Sales videos should always display populated mock data showing active calls and lead captures.
* **Do not mention third-party infrastructure terms or vendor branding:** Retell AI branding, model selectors (GPT-4.1), token counts, and API webhook configurations should remain white-labelled and hidden behind your agency's branded service offering.
* **Do not leave unclarified compliance limitations:** The video mentions lack of EU data residency (07:30). When selling to Australian clinics and businesses, ensure you speak directly to Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) and local data handling rather than highlighting foreign compliance gaps.
