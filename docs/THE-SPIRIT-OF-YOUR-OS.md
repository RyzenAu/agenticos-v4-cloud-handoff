# The spirit of your OS

Give your AI a useful place to work with you. Your OS brings a few priorities, selected context and the tools you already use into one workspace. It gets better through small, deliberate additions.

## Start with a result

Choose something you want help with this week: a daily plan, meeting preparation, finding a saved decision or turning an idea into a draft. Add only the context that makes that result possible. A connected logo is useful when it helps answer a real question.

## Eight areas, one working rhythm

### 01 · Profile: explain your world

A short description of your work, constraints and preferences helps the assistant make sensible suggestions. Keep it current. A photo and public profile links are optional; a link does not authenticate an account.

### 02 · Goals: decide what matters next

Choose a concrete weekly outcome and connect it to your longer-term direction. Make the next action small enough to do. Let the OS help you plan against a goal you actually chose.

### 03 · Dashboard: get your bearings

Use the dashboard to see your priorities and available information together. Empty cards are normal when nothing is connected. Fictional examples help you explore; they do not represent a bank balance, revenue or live audience.

### 04 · Chat: think with your context

Ask a focused question, choose a model and include the relevant sources. Ask where an answer came from. A missing source should lead to a clear gap or a question, not invented certainty. A draft or suggested action is not a completed external action.

### 05 · Memory: keep what is worth reusing

Save facts, decisions, preferences and useful documents you want to find again. Give them clear titles and keep their sources. Correct stale notes. Import a small selection before a whole archive. Source switches govern future retrieval; they do not erase text already sent in an earlier conversation.

### 06 · Inbox: turn conversations into next steps

Connect your own supported accounts when you need them. Find a message, understand its context and draft a useful response. Check recipients and wording before sending. Saved messages and live provider search have different coverage.

### 07 · Calendar: plan around reality

Look at the connected account, saved date range and last refresh. Use calendar chat to prepare or plan from that evidence. A partial calendar snapshot cannot prove you are free. Event creation is a separate reviewed action with write access.

### 08 · Create: turn a decision into something usable

Use Design, Website or agent tasks when a concrete output will help. Give a brief, inspect the result and iterate. Voice and media generation have their own setup and may have usage costs. Publishing stays a deliberate step.

## Connect, organise, reason, execute

Connect one useful source. Organise a small amount of context around a goal. Reason with it in Chat. Execute a specific, reviewable action through the tool that has the right permission. Repeat when the result earns its place in your routine.

Your Claude and Codex accounts remain yours. The OS can discover supported connections exposed by those tools. Discovery, sign-in, readable data and permission to act are distinct. When the OS cannot use a service directly, keep using it in its native app or add a supported export.

## A useful first week

On your first session, save one goal and one note, then check that Chat can use the note. The next day, connect the one source that would make your daily plan better. At the end of the week, remove stale context and keep what helped you make a decision or finish something.

You do not need every integration, a complete life archive or a perfect dashboard. Keep your setup small enough to understand and valuable enough to return to.

## Your copy is yours

The original release starts without the creator's private state. Once you add your own information, keep that installation private. Back it up before changing it. Share the original clean ZIP with someone starting out.
