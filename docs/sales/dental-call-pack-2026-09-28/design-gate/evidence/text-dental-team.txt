Book with the team

When you book online or by phone, you can choose a specific practitioner or leave it open. If you have seen someone before, we will usually suggest booking with them again for continuity, but the choice is always yours.

For same-day emergency time, we assign whoever has availability that morning, since the priority is getting you seen quickly rather than matching a preference.
