## Billing rules (all tiers)
- Minutes are counted **per second**, **added up across the month**, and rounded **up to the next whole minute once** per billing period (receptionist setting `roundingMode = PER_PERIOD`).
- Calls under 5 seconds, calls that never reach the receptionist, M&U demo and test calls don't count.
- Included minutes and SMS **reset monthly and don't roll over**.

## Fair use (all tiers)
- Inbound calls to the client's own business number(s) only; no outbound or marketing calls.
- Simultaneous-call limits above; more by arrangement (our platform concurrency is shared).
- Not an emergency service; not for clinical, legal or financial advice.

## What the customer pays for vs what we pass through
Everything above is **included** in the monthly fee: voice AI, phone number(s), carrier minutes, SMS within the allowance, hosting, support. Nothing is passed through separately unless the client asks for extras (extra numbers, extra locations, integrations), which we quote first.
