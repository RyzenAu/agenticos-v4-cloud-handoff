# Closing: asking for the decision

Close at the end of the demo, not on the cold call. Recommend **one** package from what they told you (`10-qualification.md`), not a menu.

## The recommendation
> "From what you've told me, you'd want the receptionist taking [after-hours / overflow] calls, about [N] a week, and you'd release new-patient check-ups into a booking calendar. That's **Essential**: A$699 a month + GST, 400 minutes included, and at go-live every booking lands in your connected Google Calendar or Cal.com calendar and your inbox."

**Essential's cover, before you say it:** [OWNER DECISION (a) PENDING: is Essential cover after hours and busy / no answer only, or business hours alongside the team too? Not decided.]

Swap in Professional (several practitioners, all calls alongside the team, reminders, weekly report) or Premium (multi-site, high volume, weekly call review) only when their answers point there. Prices and inclusions: `11-package-comparison.md` (generated from the catalogue; + 10% GST). Setup: quoted separately once approved.

## How we start (say it exactly)
> "Each business's booking, routing and texts are set up and tested before they go live. We run the go-live tests with you: a test booking (or a booking request, if that's your mode), an urgent call, calls through your cover and call forwarding, and a text if SMS is set up. When they pass, you confirm in writing. That's Acceptance, and it's when monthly billing and the minimum term start. Billing terms are confirmed in your agreement. Your number never changes, and you can switch call forwarding off any time."

Billing timing: [OWNER DECISION (b) PENDING: is the monthly fee billed in advance from Acceptance, or in arrears after each billing period? Not decided.]

There is **no pilot or trial offer**: don't offer one, and don't promise a setup waiver. Direct booking depends on their system: we confirm compatibility in setup, or agree a booking-request / lead workflow. Which booking mode they'll get (Booked vs Booking request) should already be settled from `10-qualification.md` before you get here — don't discover it at signing. Forwarding codes: `../receptionist-pack-2026-09-28/call-forwarding-guide.md`.

## The ask
> "Would you like me to send the proposal for [package] today, so you can look at it with [decision-maker]?"

- **Yes:** send the proposal **draft** from `../receptionist-pack-2026-09-28/proposal-template.docx` (Usman approves it first). Log **Proposal sent**. Book the follow-up call before hanging up.
- **"Need to think":** "Of course. What would you want to be sure of before saying yes?" Answer that one thing. Book a follow-up in 2 business days.
- **No:** "Thanks for the time. Can I ask what didn't fit?" Log it.

## What they sign (in order)
1. **Proposal** (package, monthly, extra minutes, the invoice example, deliverables, assumptions, timeline, acceptance; setup quoted separately once approved): `proposal-template.docx`.
2. **Service agreement**: `service-agreement-draft.docx`. **Draft only: it must have qualified legal review before any client signs it.** Don't send it until Usman confirms that review is done.
3. **Onboarding form**: `client-setup-checklist.docx`.

## Don't
- Discount to close. If price is the issue, drop a tier, not the price.
- Promise a go-live date before booking has passed acceptance on our demo line.
- Sign anyone whose calendar answer was "practice software only, no separate calendar".
